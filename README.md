# ACLS v. NEH: Inside the Grant Terminations

**[View the interactive investigation →](https://stat-guy.github.io/acls-v-neh/)**

In March–April 2025, two DOGE operatives used ChatGPT to screen 1,477 NEH grants for "DEI" involvement — then terminated nearly all of them in 22 days. This site is a data-driven analysis of the court filings, depositions, and discovery documents from *ACLS v. Wolfson* (S.D.N.Y., Case No. 25-cv-03657).

## What you'll find

- **10 Key Findings** drawn from 39 exhibits, 4 depositions, and 14 linked spreadsheets
- **Interactive Timeline** tracing the 22-day blitz from first contact to mass termination
- **Grant Explorer** — search, filter, and inspect all 2,415 reviewed grants
- **Claims vs. Evidence** — 52 identified inconsistencies between deposition testimony and documentary records
- **Data Visualizations** — termination rates by protected class, the DEI classification pipeline, and funding impact

## Source materials

All exhibit links point to the publicly available PDFs hosted by the [American Council of Learned Societies](https://www.acls.org/acls-aha-mla-lawsuit-discovery-materials/). The full [Memorandum in Support of Plaintiffs' Motion for Summary Judgment](https://www.acls.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/247-Memo-of-law-in-support-of-motion-for-summary-judgment.pdf) is also linked throughout the site.
