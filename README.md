# ACLS v. NEH: Inside the Grant Terminations

**[Read the Investigation →](https://stat-guy.github.io/acls-v-neh/)**

In March-April 2025, two DOGE operatives used ChatGPT to screen 2,415 NEH grants for "DEI" involvement — then terminated 1,477 of them in 22 days. This site is a data-driven investigation built from the court filings, depositions, and discovery documents from *ACLS v. Wolfson* (S.D.N.Y., Case No. 25-cv-03657).

## The Story

A scroll-driven investigation walks you through the case in six acts:

1. **One Grant** — A Fort Sill Apache Tribe project preserving the language of Geronimo's descendants
2. **The Wave** — Watch 1,477 dots accumulate as the termination wave sweeps across 22 days
3. **Who Decided?** — The chain of command from White House to career staff
4. **Across the Nation** — An aerial fly-over map of terminated grants in every state
5. **The Contradictions** — What they said under oath vs. what the documents show
6. **What Was Lost** — $423M in humanities funding, broken down by category

## Explore the Data

The [data explorer](https://stat-guy.github.io/acls-v-neh/explore) provides interactive tools:

- **Grant Explorer** — search, filter, and inspect all 2,415 reviewed grants
- **Key Findings** — 10 findings drawn from 39 exhibits, 4 depositions, and 14 linked spreadsheets
- **Interactive Timeline** — the 22-day blitz from first contact to mass termination
- **Claims vs. Evidence** — 52 inconsistencies between deposition testimony and documentary records
- **Data Visualizations** — termination rates by protected class, DEI classification pipeline, and funding impact
